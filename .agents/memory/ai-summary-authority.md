---
name: AI summary authority boundary
description: Durable safety rule for model-assisted supplier-risk narratives across every transport.
---

Models may select or rank approved deterministic finding identifiers, but must not author the factual narrative or any score, status, simulation flag, disposition, missing-data state, or citation.

**Why:** Validating free-form prose or checking that citations exist does not prove a claim is supported. It also allows model wording to contradict, suppress, or reinterpret deterministic findings.

**How to apply:** Render narrative and citations locally from truth-validated finding identifiers. Never send a mixed legacy/validated risk object to model-facing transports. Reject unknown IDs or extra fields, and clear stale model selections on every fallback, timeout, or failed refresh across HTTP, chat, and MCP.
