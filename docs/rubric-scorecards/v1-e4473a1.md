   explicit shared-main ref and evaluate that immutable commit. If `e4473a1` is

2. **Remediation wave:** wait until relevant owner tasks are merged. A proposed,
   active, ready, or queued task earns no points by itself.
3. **Live evidence:** decide whether judges should receive live operational
   source coverage. If yes, require current source paths/freshness and rerun;
   otherwise retain the five-point mission deduction and describe the product as
   deterministic fixture-backed decision support.
4. **Human evidence:** two presenters must complete and record the spoken
   rehearsal and handoffs. Automation cannot manufacture teamwork evidence.
5. **Organizer evidence:** either record a real successful catalog identity or
   continue saying validated dry run. Portal access cannot be inferred.
6. **Complete browser gate:** run keyboard/non-pointer, focus, accessible-name,
   responsive-width, 200% zoom, theme/contrast, failure/recovery, and complete
   download checks after their owner tasks merge.
7. **Convergence:** on the refreshed revision, rerun affected checks and the
   entire rubric. Only after **two consecutive 101/101 complete passes** agree
   may the index record rubric perfection.
